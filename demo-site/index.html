SiteScope Demo Site

This site exists so that SiteScope has an approved, predictable target to crawl. Its pages describe how a polite breadth-first crawler works, and its structure exercises the crawler: shared links, cycles, a robots.txt rule, a page that must not be indexed, a page whose links must not be followed, and a broken link.
